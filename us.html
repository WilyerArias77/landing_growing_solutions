Bookkeeping, sales tax and reporting for your U.S. business

The problem

Growing small businesses often end up with books months behind, sales tax obligations in several states that nobody is tracking, and reports that arrive too late to make decisions. For Hispanic-owned businesses, finding an accountant who explains it all in Spanish adds another hurdle.

How we solve it

We take care of your monthly bookkeeping in QuickBooks, track and prepare your sales & use tax with Avalara, and build Power BI reports so you see where your business stands. We work remotely and coordinate with your CPA or tax advisor when needed.

What you get

  • Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliations in QuickBooks
  • Sales & use tax tracking and preparation with Avalara
  • Financial reports and dashboards in Power BI
  • Month-end closing and financial statements
  • Service in English and Spanish for Hispanic-owned businesses

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with businesses in any U.S. state?

We work remotely, so we can serve businesses in different states. On the first call we review where you have sales tax obligations.

Can you work with my CPA?

Yes. We handle the day-to-day bookkeeping and sales tax, and coordinate with your CPA or tax advisor for filings that require them.

Can we talk in Spanish?

Yes. You can work with us entirely in Spanish, in English, or both.

How much does it cost?

Our services start at USD 400. The final price depends on your transaction volume and how many states you sell in, and we confirm it after a free 20-minute call.
